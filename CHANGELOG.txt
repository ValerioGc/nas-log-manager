# Changelog

All notable changes to nas-log-manager are documented in this file.

## [0.1.22]

### Fixed

- Opened from a notification without a log file, the app shows the list instead of an empty
  detail with an error.

## [0.1.21]

### Changed

- Maintenance release.

## [0.1.20]

### Changed

- The styles of the refresh and delete icons share their common rules, with the same result.

### Removed

- Comments that only named the markup below them.
- The `serde` dependency of the backend, unused: only `serde_json` is.

## [0.1.19]

### Changed

- The texts of the interface, in Italian and English, are impersonal and shorter: no ellipsis,
  colon or semicolon, no names of internal programs, commands or variables.
- The scrollbars come from the shared library, which shows only their thumb: no arrows and no
  track.

## [0.1.18]

### Changed

- The files of `assets/`, with a hash of their content in the name, are served with
  `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable`, the page with `no-cache`: the browser keeps
  the scripts and the styles between visits instead of asking for them again through the gateway
  and the launcher.
- The launcher bridge (one instance, under `src/services/nasShell.ts`), the shared styles, the page
  header, the icons, the dialog of the details and the build, package and release scripts come from
  the shared library `@valeriogc/nas-base-app`.

## [0.1.17]

### Changed

- In the frame of the launcher the application keeps the rules of its old sandbox by itself
  (`src/services/frameGuard.ts`, called first in `main.ts`): no window of its own, no
  `alert`/`confirm`/`prompt` dialog, no navigation of the page that holds it. The launcher dropped
  the sandbox, which on its origin was no wall and made the browser warn: nothing else changes.
